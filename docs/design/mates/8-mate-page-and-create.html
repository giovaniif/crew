8Mate page and New mate

Mate page has three columns: threads at left (each tagged with its source: Slack channel, DM, App or Automation), the conversation in the middle with the normal composer, and a settings rail at right. The rail holds the Environment picker (a styled Select), harness (segmented Claude / Codex / Pi, then model and effort), role, Slack, automations and memory. The rail says that changes apply at the next conversation. New mate is one composer: name on top, the role prompt as the text, and chips for harness, environment, repos and Slack. The environment chip opens a Menu with search and + New environment…. No wizard steps; role presets are suggestion chips.
