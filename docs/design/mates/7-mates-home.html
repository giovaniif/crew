7App shell and Mates home

Mates come first. The sidebar is now a fixed nav: Mates (default), Sessions, Approvals, Automations, Environments, Disk, and Settings at the bottom. The session list of the old sidebar is gone; a short list of mates sits under the nav so you can jump to one. The home is a grid of mate cards: face, name, role, status pill, the last thing it did, the harness tag and the environment badge (grey square = Local, blue square = Custom). A mate that needs you is amber, sits first, and can be approved from the card. The phone uses a bottom tab bar.
