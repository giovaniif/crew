5Settings and Servers

One Settings with one menu: the old "This app" and "Server" lists merge, so Notifications and Appearance appear once. Server-side rows carry a small server tag. Only rows that apply to the platform show: no iPhone Home Screen note and no "this browser" text in the Mac app, and no Sign out for the local server. Rows are label at left, control at right (switch, stepper, segmented, custom select). Nothing native.
