6Onboarding, connection and empty states

Onboarding asks once where agents run, sets it up with a live checklist, and adds the first workspace. That workspace becomes the default chip in the composer, so you never choose it again (this fixes "select twice"). Connection problems show as a thin banner in the sidebar, not a full screen. Empty states are one line and one action.
